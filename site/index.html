For over a decade I've worked on making speech technology trustworthy: detecting audio deepfakes and verifying who is really speaking. My teams have taken first place in international challenges, and my research spans industry and academia — Naver Clova, a postdoc at Carnegie Mellon, and now Senior Research Scientist at Apple. Today I'm increasingly focused on building speech large language models, carrying that reliability into the next generation of spoken-language systems.
